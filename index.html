#YaOk

We're #YaOk, a DJ and VJ duo based in Karlsruhe. Ashok plays DJ sets of Indian music — Bollywood, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and more — with live mridangam and dhol over the tracks. Yasha performs live visuals and plays sitar. We play festivals, club nights, weddings and cultural events in Germany and India.

Yasha

Live coding · visuals · sitar

Yasha is a live coding visual artist and one half of the duo — the visuals you see are written as code and performed live, right there in the room. She works as a new media artist by day. Off work she plays the sitar and loves hosting DJ parties.

DJ · mridangam · ghatam · kanjira

  • You bring A sturdy table, two power sockets, and a sound system that can take low end.
